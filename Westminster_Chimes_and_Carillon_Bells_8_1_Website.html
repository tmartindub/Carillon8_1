A substantial portable-edition update

What's new in version 8.1

The familiar automatic bell system remains, with practical improvements focused on scheduling accuracy, easier maintenance, and clearer day-to-day operation.

01

More precise scheduling

Scheduled times retain seconds, display in familiar 12-hour time, and rebuild cleanly after playlist or maintenance changes.

02

Schedule-aware silence

Up to twelve recurring silence windows prevent events from being scheduled during quiet periods while system mute remains available as protection.

03

Seven annual music rotations

Choose a default random-music folder plus six dated overrides that return automatically every year.

04

Clearer operation screens

Larger playlist text, improved time entry, labeled navigators, theme-aware schedules, and readable status information simplify regular use.

05

Built-in activity review

Open the Carillon log inside the program, review recent activity, and use optional morning email reports for operational oversight.

06

Guided v7.2.4 conversion

A separate converter backs up and upgrades an existing v7.2.4 database for the v8.1 schedule, silence, and rotation features.

Complete the outdoor bell system

Essential hardware

Carillon 8.1 works with standard Windows audio equipment and can usually be connected to hardware already in place. Consult an audio professional to match components and safely configure outdoor coverage.

01 / Outdoor speakers

Weather-resistant horn speakers

Use one to four suitable outdoor units to provide clear coverage around the church or organization.

02 / Amplification

Properly matched amplifier

Select an amplifier capable of driving the installed speakers clearly and reliably at the required distance.

03 / Connectivity

Durable wiring and distribution

Use appropriate speaker wire and any needed splitters or controls for local monitoring and outdoor playback.

Keep the equipment you already own

Upgrade the software, not the whole bell system

Westminster Chimes and Carillon Bells works with most Windows sound cards and conventional amplifiers and speakers. Organizations replacing older bell software can often keep their existing audio hardware.

Portable and free

No traditional installer is required. The application runs from a USB flash drive, stores its database and supporting files with the program, and remains free to use and share with no subscription or premium tier.
