Simple portable setup

After downloading

ZIP files must be extracted before use. Do not run Carillon or music directly from inside a compressed ZIP file.

  1. Step 1Download the selected software, guide, or music collection to the computer.
  2. Step 2Right-click the ZIP file and choose Extract All.
  3. Step 3Copy the extracted Carillon files to the root of the USB flash drive. Add optional music to suitable folders on that drive.
  4. Step 4Open the flash drive and start Carillon there using start_carillon.vbs or Carillon.exe.
