Westminster Chimes and Carillon Bells v8.1 Portable Flash Drive Package

Download all three ZIP files from the GitHub release and extract them to the
root of the flash drive. Allow Windows to merge folders when extracting the
second and third ZIP files.

1. Westminster_Chimes_and_Carillon_Bells_v8_1_Portable_1_Core.zip
2. Westminster_Chimes_and_Carillon_Bells_v8_1_Portable_2_Songs_A.zip
3. Westminster_Chimes_and_Carillon_Bells_v8_1_Portable_3_Songs_B.zip
